Our Story

Built on the yard, run on our word.

Harley Transport started as a single vehicle out of Weston-super-Mare. We've grown by doing the unglamorous thing well: turning up, on time, every time.

Where We Started

A South West haulier that still answers its own phone

We're based at Unit 74, Gazelle Road in Weston-super-Mare — close enough to the motorway network to reach the South West and South Wales fast, small enough that you still speak to the same people every time you call.

Every vehicle in our fleet is ours. Every driver is employed, trained and vetted by us. That's a deliberate choice: it means when something needs to flex — an earlier collection, a last-minute drop, a different vehicle size — we can make the call ourselves instead of chasing a subcontractor for an answer.

It's also why we can stand behind a 0% damage-or-loss record on our multi-day service for the last 3.5 years. That number isn't a marketing line; it's the result of the same drivers running the same routes, and caring whether the job is done right.

What We Stand For

The values behind every job sheet

Reliability

If we say the hour, we mean the hour. Our ETA is a commitment, not an estimate.

Doing Our Bit

Lower-impact haulage, mile by mile

Route planning

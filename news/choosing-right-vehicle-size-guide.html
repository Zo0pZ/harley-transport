7.5t, 18t or 44t? A straight-talking guide to choosing the right vehicle

Booking the wrong size vehicle wastes money either way. Here's how to get it right first time.

Guides6 min readPublished by Harley Transport

Get the vehicle size wrong and you either overpay for capacity you didn't need, or you turn up with a vehicle that can't physically take the load. Here's a quick, honest guide to matching tonnage to the job.

7.5 tonne — local, urgent and tight access

Our 7.5 tonne vehicles are the go-to for same-day work, local multi-drop rounds, and anywhere access is restricted — town centres, retail units, residential sites. If the job is a handful of pallets or less and needs to move fast, this is usually the right size.

12–18 tonne — the scheduled workhorse

For regular trade deliveries and pallet network work, our mid-weight rigids carry meaningfully more than a 7.5t without the access restrictions of a full artic. This is the size most of our scheduled, recurring contracts run on.

25 tonne — bulk without the full trailer

When a job has real volume but doesn't justify a 44 tonne artic — or the drop-off site can't accommodate one — a 25 tonne heavy rigid gives you most of the payload with more flexibility on access.

44 tonne — full loads and trunk routes

For full trailer loads, bulk freight, and overnight trunk routes between depots, 44 tonne artics are the most efficient option per pallet moved. This is also what we run on our through-the-night multi-drop services.

Not sure? Tell us the load, not the vehicle

The most common mistake is booking by vehicle size instead of by load. Tell us the dimensions, weight, number of pallets and access restrictions at both ends, and we'll recommend — and quote — the right vehicle. It's usually cheaper than guessing.
